# AI use, human responsibility and verification limits

Reviewed November 3, 2026. This account distinguishes RGDS's AI-assistance requirements and illustrative records from evidence about how the research and repository were produced. It adds no deployment, approval, field-effectiveness or validation claim.

## Policy and illustrative use

The [AI assistance policy](ai-assistance-policy.md) specifies the information a decision record should preserve when AI assists with preparation or analysis. It reserves decision authority, approvals and risk acceptance to people. Several policy requirements exceed the schema and semantic checks, as the policy itself explains.

The repository contains [six illustrative decision records](research-summary.md). [Example 0006](../examples/rgds-dec-0006-ai-assisted-conditional-go.json) models comparison, extraction and drafting with a “ChatGPT-style assistant”; its version is expressly unpinned. The example includes review and disposition entries. These authored entries describe the example's workflow and do not independently establish that an institutional review or approval occurred. The [examples guide](../examples/README.md#ai-usage-in-examples) likewise states that declarations of no AI use do not independently verify authorship.

## Responsibility and evidence limits

A policy stating that human review is mandatory does not demonstrate that a particular source was read or a review completed. Structural and semantic validation checks required fields and specified relationships. Substantive source checking and the quality of a decision require separate evidence.

The inspected README, AI policy, example and research account do not provide a retrospective task-level inventory of AI assistance used to develop RGDS research, prose, code or tests. They do not establish an exact tool/model history or a complete record of adopted and rejected outputs and actual human checks. This is a documentation limit, not a claim that assistance was absent.

The responsible researcher remains accountable for adopted work. Future material assistance should identify the tool and model where known, date, task, affected artifact, adoption or correction decision, and human check actually performed. Keep illustrative review fields, actual review events and AI-assisted critique separately attributed.

## Reporting record

The [program AI-use statement](https://nodeandnorm.org/about/research-integrity/#ai-use) provides shared reporting principles; the versioned project records describe the assistance and review documented here.

This November 3 disclosure note was prepared with Codex (OpenAI) assistance through source-record inspection and drafting. That documentation work does not establish independent human verification of the earlier research.
