# Regulated Gate Decision Support: findings, use and next steps

Reviewed 29 October 2026. This account synthesizes the linked records at source revision `a217a57490f357aa06c509abd821592370556a7d`. It adds no validation claim, release decision or change to the underlying method.

Status: Reference implementation. Evidence: Six illustrative records.

## Question and contribution

Why did a team proceed, stop or wait, and what evidence must close the conditions attached to its permission?

A schema, validators and six illustrative decision records that make permission, unresolved conditions and closure evidence explicit.

## Findings to date

The records demonstrate conditional go, no-go, defer and regulated preparation scenarios. Validation checks required structure and selected relationships, including conditional-approval requirements and AI-assistance disclosures.

The v2.0.1 correction brought the two validator entry points’ semantic checks together and enabled date-format checking. Records previously accepted through those validation gaps may fail after correction; the six canonical records and schema contract were preserved.

## How to use the work

Design conditional approvals, assign follow-up ownership and inspect whether evidence gaps and permission boundaries are recorded.

## Current status and why

The reviewed core remains a working reference implementation at v2.0.1. The historical independent study has a separate version and DOI. No closeout decision or demonstrated institutional benefit is inferred for the implementation.

## Limits and next evidence

Six authored examples and successful software checks do not establish actual IND submission outcomes, regulatory acceptance, faster reconstruction or field effectiveness. Recorded owners and closure requirements still require substantive review.

Evaluate reconstruction and condition handling in a defined institutional setting, using independent review and observed workflow evidence before claiming operational benefit.

## Research origins

Syner-G BioPharma Group’s [Navigating Successful IND Submissions](https://synergbiopharma.com/webinar/navigating-successful-ind-submissions/) webinar informs the work through practitioner accounts of late reports, conflicting source values and decisions to pause. The repository also describes other public discussions and operational interviews as design signals. These reported professional experiences remain separate from the authored illustrative records and from evidence of RGDS deployment.
